# 🔥 Unreal Trainer Certification Question Scoring Rubric (0–100)

## Score Tiers

### 90–100 — Exceptional ⭐

- ✅ Flawless grammar
- ✅ Perfect clarity
- ✅ Fully accurate UE5 technical content
- ✅ Strong distractors, no giveaways
- ✅ Reads like an official Epic certification question
- ✅ No ambiguous wording whatsoever
- ✅ Difficulty level matches content

### 80–89 — Very Good 🟢

- ✅ Strong writing with minor polish needed
- ✅ Distractors acceptable but maybe slightly uneven
- ✅ Solid technical grounding
- ✅ No major clarity issues

### 70–79 — Good 🟡

- ⚠️ Understandable but contains noticeable issues
- ⚠️ Distractors may be weak or uneven
- ⚠️ Slight grammar inconsistencies
- ⚠️ May be too easy, too hard, or slightly ambiguous

### 60–69 — Adequate 🟠

- ⚠️ Usable question but needs significant rewriting
- ⚠️ Ambiguous phrasing
- ⚠️ Technical relevance is borderline
- ⚠️ Distractors weak or obvious
- ⚠️ Grammar issues soften clarity

### 50–59 — Weak 🔴

- ❌ Multiple clarity problems
- ❌ Distractors very poor
- ❌ Not certification-quality
- ❌ Possibly misleading or technically shallow

### 40–49 — Poor 🔴

- ❌ Serious clarity/grammar problems
- ❌ Incorrect or confusing technical framing
- ❌ Distractors non-functional

### Below 40 — Unacceptable ⛔

- ❌ Fundamentally flawed question
- ❌ Incorrect UE facts
- ❌ Ambiguous or misleading
- ❌ Completely broken structure

---

## Usage in Application

- **Automatic Filtering**: Questions scoring below 70 should be flagged for review
- **Batch Export**: Only questions scoring 75+ should be included in training data exports
- **Visual Indicators**: Color-code questions based on score tier in the UI
- **Human Review**: All questions require human approval regardless of AI score (Human-in-the-Loop principle)
